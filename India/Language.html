Popular Languages Of India

India is home to hundreds of languages. Here are five of the most widely spoken across the nation

Hindi

The most widely spoken language in India and one of the official languages of the country.

Bengali

Spoken in West Bengal and Bangladesh, Bengali is known for its rich cultural and literary heritage.

Telugu

A Dravidian language spoken mainly in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, known for its classical literature.

Marathi

Spoken widely in Maharashtra, Marathi has a long tradition of poetry and theater.

Tamil

One of the oldest living languages, spoken in Tamil Nadu and parts of Sri Lanka.
