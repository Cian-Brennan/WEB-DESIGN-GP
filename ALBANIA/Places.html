Places in Albania
Albania is known for its shockingly beautiful nature, offering everything from rugged alpine mountains and deep canyons to pristine beaches, waterfalls, and untouched national parks. Beyond its natural wonders, Albania’s cities bring a unique character of their own — each one rich with history, culture, traditions, and vibrant street life. From the colorful capital of Tirana to the stone towns of Berat and Gjirokastër, every city tells a different story and invites visitors with warm hospitality.
Top Nature Spots in Albania
  • Theth National Park – A stunning alpine valley known for the Blue Eye spring, waterfalls, and scenic hiking trails.
  • Valbona Valley – Surrounded by dramatic mountain peaks, perfect for nature lovers and hikers.
  • Llogara Pass – A high mountain pass offering breathtaking views of where the mountains meet the Ionian Sea.
  • Gjipe Canyon – A spectacular canyon with a secluded beach, great for exploring and photography.
  • Blue Eye (Syri i Kaltër) – A deep, crystal-blue natural spring surrounded by lush forest near Sarandë.
  • Lake Ohrid (Pogradec) – One of Europe’s oldest lakes with serene waters and peaceful nature spots.
Top Landmarks
  • Berat Castle – A historic hilltop fortress overlooking the UNESCO-listed “City of a Thousand Windows.”
  • Gjirokastër Castle – One of the largest castles in the Balkans with panoramic views and medieval architecture.
  • Butrint National Park – A UNESCO site featuring Greek, Roman, and medieval ruins in a beautiful natural setting.
  • Apolonia Archaeological Park – An ancient city with temples, arches, and stunning countryside surroundings.
  • Rozafa Castle – A legendary fortress with dramatic views over Shkodër’s rivers and landscape.
  • Skanderbeg Square – Tirana’s central square, surrounded by museums and colorful modern architecture.
Top Cities in Albania
  • Tirana – Colorful capital city full of museums, cafes, street art, and the Dajti Mountain cable car.
  • Berat – UNESCO town famous for its Ottoman houses and hillside Berat Castle.
  • Gjirokastër – The “Stone City,” known for its stone roofs, old bazaar, and impressive castle.
  • Shkodër – One of the oldest cities in Albania, home to Rozafa Castle and a lively artistic culture.
  • Korçë – Known for its charming streets, the Old Bazaar, and its strong cultural identity.
  • Sarandë – A beautiful coastal city on the Albanian Riviera, famous for beaches and nearby Ksamil.
